Updated 9 November 2026

Put a widget on your Home Screen

  1. Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen until the apps jiggle.
  2. Tap Edit, then Add Widget. Search for Widgy, choose Countdown, To-Do, Note or Photo, pick a size and tap Add Widget.
  3. Touch and hold the new widget, tap Edit Widget and choose which of your Widgy widgets it shows.

For the Lock Screen: touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, then tap the widget area under the time. Countdown, To-Do and Note have Lock Screen sizes.

Restore Premium

Open Widgy, tap the gear in the top corner, then Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

Cancel or change a subscription

In Widgy Settings tap Manage subscription, or open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions.
