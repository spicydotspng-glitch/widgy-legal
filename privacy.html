Privacy Policy

Updated 9 November 2026

The short version

Widgy does not collect, store or share any personal information. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your device

The widgets you make (titles, dates, notes, to-do items) and any photo you choose for a widget are saved only on your device, in storage shared between the Widgy app and its widgets. Photos are resized for the widget and never leave your device. Deleting a widget deletes its photo; deleting the app deletes everything.

Widgy reads only the photos you pick in the system photo picker. It never sees the rest of your library and asks for no photo permission.

Purchases

Widgy Premium is a subscription sold by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles payment; Widgy only receives confirmation from the App Store that a subscription is active, on your device. We never see your payment details.

Children

Widgy does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes and contact
